
# Women Empowerment Job Portal (WEJP)

## Project Overview

The Women Empowerment Job Portal is an online platform designed to bridge the gap between women seeking employment opportunities and organizations that prioritize gender diversity. Accessible via both mobile and computer browsers, the portal aims to empower women by providing easy access to job listings, career resources, and professional development tools.

## Objectives

* To understand and explain the main functionalities of the portal.


* To describe the key features and purposes of the various modules available to users.


* To highlight the roles and privileges of different users within the system.


* To outline the complete workflow of the portal, ensuring a seamless user experience.



## Main Modules

* **Login Module:** Allows users to log in based on their roles and privileges to access relevant features.


* **Registration Module:** Enables new job seekers to create profiles and apply for jobs, and employers to register organizations and post job openings.


* **Administration Module:** Oversees the entire portal, managing user accounts, monitoring activity, and updating job postings.



## System Users

* **Job Seeker:** Women looking for job opportunities who can create profiles, search and apply for jobs, and track applications.


* **Employer:** Organizations or businesses looking to hire women, create/manage job listings, review applications, and communicate with candidates.


* **Admin:** Has full control to manage user accounts, approve/reject job listings, and monitor system performance.
